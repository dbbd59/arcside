Arcside — installation

1. Extract arcside-chrome-<version>.zip into a folder you won't move (for example ~/Applications/arcside).
2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on "Developer mode".
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the extracted folder.
4. Pin the Arcside icon to the toolbar and click it to open the side panel.

Notes
- The extension ID depends on the folder path: if you move it or load it from another place, Chrome treats it as a new extension and your data (Spaces, folders, saved tabs) doesn't follow.
  To move it: Settings -> Backup -> Export JSON, then Import… in the new install.
- Arcside saves a daily backup to Downloads/Arcside backups/.
- Shortcuts: Cmd+Shift+D opens the panel, Cmd+Shift+C copies the link, Cmd+Shift+K opens the in-page command bar.
  Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
